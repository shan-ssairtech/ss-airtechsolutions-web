About us

A compressed air business built on service

SS AirTech Solutions is an industrial compressed air company based in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. We are an authorised dealer for Sollant screw air compressors and the manufacturer of the AT series of reciprocating compressors. We sell, install and service all of it.

Who we are

Our work covers the whole life of a compressed air installation: sizing the right machine for the application, supplying the tank, dryer, filters and piping, commissioning it on site, and keeping it running with genuine spare parts and maintenance visits.

Customers come from automotive, textile, food and beverage, pharmaceutical, engineering and plastics manufacturing. Every quotation is prepared by an engineer against your actual duty cycle, not a catalogue guess.
